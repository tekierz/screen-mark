# ACT ONE

## INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY #1#

<!-- @prop: espresso machine, rag, iced coffee @wardrobe: apron @sound: espresso hiss -->

A cramped third-wave coffee shop. Plants everywhere. MAYA (30s, apron, permanent scowl) wipes down the counter, eyeing the door.

The espresso machine hisses like it holds a grudge.

**MAYA**
_(under her breath)_
> He's late. Again.

The door chimes. JONAS (30s, helmet hair, bike messenger bag) bursts in, breathless.

**JONAS**
> Traffic. I swear on my mother's sourdough starter.

**MAYA**
> You bike here.

<!-- TODO: tighten this exchange — too much banter before the reveal -->

They speak over each other:

**MAYA**
> Every single shift, Jonas. Every one.

**JONAS** ^
> There was a parade! A whole parade!

**MAYA**
_(sliding a cup across)_
> Your usual. Decaf.

**JONAS**
> That's cold.

**MAYA**
> It's iced.

~ Cold brew, cold heart, that's the way the mornings start...

@CUT TO:

## EXT. STREET - CONTINUOUS #2#

<!-- @prop: bicycle, iced coffee @vfx: none @animal: pigeon -->

Jonas pedals furiously through traffic, one hand on the bars, the other clutching the iced coffee like a torch.

A PIGEON judges him from a lamppost.
